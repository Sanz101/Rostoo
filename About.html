About Rosto

We are dedicated to delivering premier, private travel and transport experiences across Sri Lanka. Our mission is to combine local expertise with absolute comfort, ensuring every journey—from scenic hill country paths to airport transfers—exceeds your expectations.

Our story

Rosto began with a simple idea: travelers exploring Kandy deserve a driver and guide they can trust completely, not just for a ride, but for the whole journey.

Years on the Road

Founded by Ranjan, who has guided travelers through the hill country for over a decade.

A Trusted Network

Close partnerships with hotels, adventure parks, and local guides across the region.

Care in Every Detail

From the first WhatsApp message to the final drop-off, every trip is planned around you.
