Featured Destinations

Explore breathtaking tourist hotspots located around Kandy, perfectly positioned for easy day trips or extended highland tours with Ranjan.

Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

Located right in Kandy (0 km)

Sri Lanka's most sacred Buddhist shrine, nestled by the scenic Kandy Lake featuring gorgeous traditional architecture and rich cultural heritage.

Sigiriya Rock Fortress

Approx. 90 km from Kandy (2.5 hours drive)

An ancient palace and fortress complex sitting atop a massive 200-meter-high rock column surrounded by stunning water gardens and frescoes.

Nuwara Eliya & Tea Gardens

Approx. 75 km from Kandy (2.5 hours drive)

Known as 'Little England', featuring cascading waterfalls, misty green hills, and sprawling world-famous Ceylon tea plantations.

Kitulgala Adventure Base

Approx. 55 km from Kandy (1.5 hours drive)

The premier location for white water rafting, rainforest trekking, and outdoor adventure sports along the Kelani River.
